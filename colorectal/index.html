Colorectal Tumor Segmentation of CT Scans Based on a Convolutional Neural Network With an Attention Mechanism

Yun Pei*, Lin Mu*, Yu Fu, Kan He, Hong Li, Shuxu Guo, Xiaoming Liu, Mingyang Li, Huimao Zhang, Xueyan Li

IEEE Access 2020 · * Equal contribution

IEEE Access PDF

The study evaluates a convolutional encoder–decoder that predicts tumor masks on CT slices using attention across pixel positions and feature channels.

From CT slice to tumor mask

  1. Encode a 512 × 512 CT slice into feature maps.
  2. Combine position attention, channel attention and local encoder features.
  3. Decode the fused features through upsampling and skip connections to predict a tumor mask at the input resolution.

The model uses a Dice loss to train against masks annotated by two radiologists and checked by a senior radiologist.

Segmentation results

The study used 1,131 CT slices from 158 patients at the First Hospital of Jilin University. It allocated 904 slices to training and 227 to testing.

Takeaway The dual-attention network reached a Dice score of 70.89%, up 1.46 percentage points from U-Net and 0.66 from CE-Net. CE-Net had the highest precision.
